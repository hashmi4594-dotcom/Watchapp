Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Hashmi Watchapp ("we", "our") is a website where signed-in users watch videos and earn coins. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile ID, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • Activity: how long you watch videos and the coins you earn.
  • Payout details: if you request a payout, the UPI ID, phone number and WhatsApp number you enter. We use them only to pay you and to contact you about the payment.
  • On your device: your browser stores sign-in session data and, for admins, a security key. We do not use this to track you across other sites.

How we use it

To run your account, count watch time, process payouts, prevent misuse and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

We show our own video ads and may also show ads from advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party service providers for sign-in, database, video storage and hosting. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. To ask for your data to be deleted, email us from your registered address and we will respond within a reasonable time. Payment records may be kept as required by law.

Children

This service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
